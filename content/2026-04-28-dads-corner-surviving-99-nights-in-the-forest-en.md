## Hey Kids, It's Dad Again

FaionEnder here, reporting live from the campfire. My daughter EnderFaion finally dragged me into another co-op run of *99 Nights in the Forest*, and let me tell you — 99 nights? In my day we called that "a school week." Cue the groan. I'll see myself out.

But seriously, this game has become our favorite weekend hangout, and I think a lot of you would love it too. So grab a snack, pull up a log, and let me share what's going on in the spookiest forest on Roblox.

## What's the Game About?

If you somehow missed it, *99 Nights in the Forest* is a co-op survival game on Roblox where up to 4 players team up to protect a younger sibling NPC (everyone calls her "Sister") inside a campfire circle. You forage by day, defend by night, and try not to lose your wood supply when the wolves show up.

The vibe is spooky but soft — think shadowy trees, glowing eyes, crackling fire. No gore, no scary jumpscares, just that cozy "don't let the fire go out" tension that reminds me of the survival games I played as a kid.

## Why Families Are Loving It

This is one of those rare Roblox games that actually rewards teamwork between a parent and a kid. EnderFaion handles the fast stuff — chopping logs, scouting berries, dodging bears. I sit by the campfire, keep Sister warm, and yell helpful things like "WATCH OUT BEHIND YOU" two seconds too late.

The map keeps growing too. There's a snow region, a swampy area, and the deeper woods where the really weird shadow creatures live. Every run feels a little different, and the crafting tree (axe → spear → bow → traps) keeps you planning ahead instead of just clicking buttons.

## Dad's Three Patience Tips

Here's the wisdom your old man wants to pass down:

1. **Stockpile berries on nights 1 through 3.** The first big creature wave hits fast. If your hunger meter is empty, you're toast.
2. **Always carry a torch at night.** Wolves hate fire. So do bears. So does my electric bill, but that's a different story.
3. **Upgrade the campfire BEFORE the cool weapons.** I know, I know, the bow looks awesome. But if Sister freezes, game over. Survival beats style.

## The Real Lesson

Patience is a survival stat — in the forest, and honestly, in real life too. Rushing gets you eaten. Planning gets you to night 100.

Now if you'll excuse me, EnderFaion just messaged me "Dad you died again 💀" so I have to go reclaim my dignity. See you at the campfire!
